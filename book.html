Claim Your Crown

On ambition, reinvention and the courage to build a life on your own terms.

From Chapter Six

Success. A word we’ve all been taught to chase, but rarely taught to define.

Let’s breathe together before we begin. Inhale through your nose. Exhale slowly through your mouth. Now ask yourself, not from the mind, but from your spirit: what does success feel like? Not look like. Feel like, to me?

Because here’s the thing. Success is not one-size-fits-all. It’s not a zip code. It’s not a salary. It’s not a relationship status. It’s not a verified check. It’s not what they post. It’s not what your parents hoped. It’s not what your peers applaud.

Success, real, soul-fed success, is deeply personal. And if you’re not careful, you’ll spend your entire life climbing a ladder that was never built for you, only to reach the top and realize you don’t even like the view. You hate it here.

This chapter is your permission slip to redefine everything. To step out of survival mode. To get off autopilot. And to ask, in the rawest, most radical way: what does success look like in my language? In my body? In my rhythm?

Because when you reclaim the definition, you reclaim the destination. And suddenly you’re not just winning. You’re winning on your own terms.

“Claim Your Crown gives creators what they actually need: the inner armor, mental toughness and practical and actionable tools to help them claim their crown.”

Robert A. Celestin, Esq.
