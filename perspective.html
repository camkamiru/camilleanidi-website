Counsel’s Note 01 · August 2026

You Can’t Google What You Don’t Know

Copyright doesn’t wait for you. It doesn’t wait for a release date or a deal or a lawyer. The second that song exists in a form you can hear, rights exist. And if two people made it together and never wrote down what they agreed to, the law will make an assumption for you, and the assumption it makes is almost never the one you had in your head that night in the apartment. The law fills in your silence. And it fills it in with default rules that were written by people who have never met you, never heard your record, and do not care what you meant.
