Practice

Practice

Recording and distribution, music publishing, intellectual property and licensing, and international entertainment transactions.

Recording & Distribution

Recording agreements, label relationships, distribution arrangements, artist and producer agreements, and related commercial terms.

Music Publishing

Songwriter agreements, publishing administration, co-publishing, rights ownership, royalty structures and catalog matters.

Intellectual Property & Licensing

Copyright, music rights, licensing, clearances and the commercial exploitation of intellectual property.

International Entertainment Transactions

Cross-border negotiations, international partnerships, commercial structures and the legal considerations affecting artists and music businesses operating in multiple jurisdictions.

Camille is admitted to practice in New York. Her international work is transactional and does not include advising on the local law of other jurisdictions. Legal inquiries are handled through The Law Offices of Robert A. Celestin.

Play to Win

Lagos, 2024 to 2025. Presented by The Law Offices of Robert A. Celestin in partnership with EMPIRE Africa.

An annual music business initiative bringing together artists, music executives and industry professionals through live auditions, legal education, panel discussions and artist development programming.
