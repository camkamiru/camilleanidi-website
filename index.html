About

Where law
meets culture
and power.

I am an international entertainment and IP attorney based between Lagos and New York. As Associate Attorney at The Law Offices of Robert A. Celestin, I represent global artists navigating the business of music across Africa, the U.S., and beyond.

My roster includes BNXN fka Buju, Seyi Vibez, Vector, Sabri, and Shaboozey. My work spans contract negotiation, publishing strategy, catalog protection, and cross-border deal structuring. Named to Billboard's Top Music Lawyers list three consecutive years — 2023, 2024, and 2025.

My mission is simple: make sure international artists don't just go viral — they stay protected. I translate complex IP and deal structures into strategies that creatives can understand, apply, and build with.
